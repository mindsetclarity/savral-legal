Viral Saver — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Viral Saver is a bookmarking app. You save links to posts you like, and we help you find them again. This page says exactly what we store and what we don't.

What we store

  • Your account: the email address your Google or Apple sign-in gives us, and a user id. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email", we only ever see the relay address.
  • Your saves: the link you shared, the post's public title, caption, creator name and a copy of its thumbnail image, the categories and keywords our sorting assigns, and any note you type.
  • Your country, taken from your phone's region setting, so we can hide platforms that are blocked where you are. We never ask for or read your location.
  • Minimal usage events: the type (save, search, open, export), the time, and the id of the save involved. Nothing else.

What we do not do

  • We do not download or store videos. We store a link and a thumbnail.
  • We do not use your login to any other app, and never ask for one.
  • We do not show ads, and we do not sell or share your data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We use no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.

Who else touches your data

  • Supabase (our database and servers, hosted in Singapore) stores your account and saves.
  • OpenAI receives the post's title, caption, creator, and your note, in order to assign categories and keywords. It is not used to train their models under our API terms.
  • Sentry receives crash reports. Links, notes and email addresses are removed from those reports before they are sent.
  • The post's own platform (Instagram, YouTube and so on) sees an ordinary public request for the post's details.

Deleting your data

Open the app, go to Settings → Delete account. This removes your saves, your thumbnails and your account from our database, permanently, right away. You can also export everything first from Settings → Export, free, as CSV or JSON. If you can't reach the app, email us and we will delete it for you.

Children

Viral Saver is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. In India, verified parental consent for users under 18 applies from 13 May 2027; an age check will ship before that date.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what we store, we will update this page and note it in the app's release notes.
